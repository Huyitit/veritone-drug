getSignedWritableAssetUrl(
assetId: ID!
expiresInSeconds: Int
): SignedWritableAssetUrlInfo

Issue a short-lived signed PUT URL to overwrite an EXISTING asset in place.
The destination bucket + key are derived server-side from the persisted asset
(never from client input) and confined to buckets managed by this service, so
the URL can only overwrite that specific asset object. Intended for internal
platform components (e.g. core-eventing) to flush asset content without holding
S3 credentials of their own. The returned contentType and, when non-null,
serverSideEncryption are baked into the signature — the caller MUST send
matching Content-Type and x-amz-server-side-encryption headers on the PUT.
Restricted to internal service tokens and (currently) media-mdp assets.
